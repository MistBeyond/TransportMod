# Tracks

Status: `planned` (placeholder; content pending)

Parent overview: `docs/roadmap/rail.md`

## Summary

Tracks are the foundation of the railway network. They provide train paths, rail sections, and the player-facing placement experience.

## Scope

- Real-world track models and widths as references.
- Track block scale, gauge, and visual presentation.
- Placement and connectivity for straight track, curves, slopes, and intersections.
- Enhanced block placement and Satisfactory-like ghost preview.
- Connected or intersecting tracks forming rail sections.

## Open Questions

- How real-world gauge scales to Minecraft block dimensions.
- How placement interaction can be convenient and predictable.
- What rules curves, slopes, and intersections use.
- How this system relates to and differs from vanilla Minecraft rails.
