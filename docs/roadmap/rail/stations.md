## Summary

Stations handle train stopping, cargo loading and unloading, and interaction with the railway network. They are an important boundary for freight gameplay.

## Scope

- Station form and placement.
- Train stopping detection and station matching.
- Item and fluid loading/unloading systems.
- Cargo source, destination, and capacity rules.
- Station interaction with dispatch and signals.

## Open Questions

- Whether stations use single-block, multi-block, track-mounted, or modular structures.
- How transfer speed and simultaneous loading/unloading are defined.
- Whether stations participate in rail section or signal logic.
- How stations interact with freight underframes and replaceable cargo bodies.
